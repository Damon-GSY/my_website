# Handoff identity bible

- Exactly one orange-and-white articulated arm, one small apricot parcel and one low mobile carrier. Keep dimensions, arm joint count and parcel markings stable.
- The gripper begins above the parcel, descends, closes around its sides and lifts it only a few centimeters. The parcel must remain attached to the gripper after contact, with believable weight and shadows.
- The carrier, white warehouse modules, conveyor, planted details and cobalt/apricot route lights stay fixed under one locked isometric camera.
- Keep the active bay center-right and left 35 percent quiet lavender for HTML copy.
- No worker, extra robot, duplicated parcel, signage, letters, numbers, logos, buttons or metrics baked into the imagery.
- Inspect contact and occlusion frame by frame; reverse scrub must naturally lower and release the same parcel.
