# Matter identity bible

- Exactly one continuous polished chrome object. K0 is a rounded pebble or droplet; K1 is the same mass shaped as a thick sculptural capital D with one open cavity.
- Preserve mirror finish, thickness, studio cobalt hue, three-quarter camera, studio floor position, light direction and grounded contact shadow.
- Keep the object center-right. The left 35 percent is quiet background for editable page text.
- No second D, detached fragments, sparks, floating particles, engraved words, logos or UI in either keyframe or the clip.
- Inspect every intermediate frame for conserved apparent mass, a single coherent surface and a cavity that opens progressively rather than suddenly appearing.
- The motion must read both forward and backward and hold cleanly at any intermediate frame.
