What the visitor does

Scrolls. Each notch commits one token; the alternatives hover in depth with their odds, then press flat. Scrolling back un-writes the page.

Why it is his

He trains language agents. Showing the choices behind every word is a portfolio that is also a lesson in how a model decides.

Main risk

Reads as a gimmick if slow. Text must be instantly complete for skimmers and screen readers; the generation is a layer, never a gate.
