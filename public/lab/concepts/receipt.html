What the visitor does

Scrolls and hears the page print. Each project lands as a line item with its number; at the bottom they tear off the stub to get in touch.

Why it is his

He reports results as figures: 90% fewer misoperations, 530 samples, 100+ tools. A receipt is the format where every line has to add up.

Main risk

Cute can read as cheap. It needs restraint: one printer, real thermal typography, no sound by default, and a plain list underneath.
