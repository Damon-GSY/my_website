AWhat the visitor does

Scrolls past the end of a section; the page balls up, rolls aside and opens flat as the next one. Every section carries the creases of the last transition.

BWhy it is his

A printed sheet that has been worked on: notes, drafts and evaluations rather than a polished brochure. It reads as someone who prints things and marks them up.

CMain risk

Reading comfort. Crease shading on body text can look like a rendering bug; it must stay under a few percent and never cross small type.
