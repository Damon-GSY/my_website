What the visitor does

Scrolls through a small white city, then watches it level into a chart. Hovering a tower names the project behind the number.

Why it is his

He measures things. Here the evidence is the architecture: 530 samples, ~250 papers, 100+ tools, built to scale, nothing decorative.

Main risk

Six numbers spanning 3 to 530 need a log scale, and log scales mislead when unlabelled. The axis must stay visible in every frame.
