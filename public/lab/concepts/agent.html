Frame 3

The visit, laid flat as a trace

The sheets collapse into one readable page: every step with its tier and outcome. The visitor leaves with the trace, not a sitemap.

Layers apart · agent on top Sheet rising · Tier 3 gate Flat · one readable trace
AWhat the visitor does

Watches the site navigate for them, can take the wheel at any point, and is asked before anything leaves the page. At the end they can read back exactly what happened.

BWhy it is his

It is his job on screen: train an agent, bound its authority, then read the trace. The three principles become the interaction model, not a slide.

CMain risk

Hijacking the visit. If the agent moves before the visitor has oriented, it feels like an ad; it needs a clear "take over" and a calm default pace.
