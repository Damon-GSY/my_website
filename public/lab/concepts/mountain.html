AWhat the visitor does

Lands on a landscape, scrolls, and the camera climbs. The ball follows the scroll through the valleys and comes to rest as the full stop of his name.

BWhy it is his

A name you read as ground to be surveyed: terrain is measured, not admired. It sets up "Evaluate before training" before a word of it is read.

CMain risk

Legibility at the start. Too much relief and DAMON GUAN reads as noise; the heightfield has to stay faithful to the letterforms at every angle.
