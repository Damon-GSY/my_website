AWhat the visitor does

Browses a shelf instead of a list, picks a carton by its number, and watches it open into the write-up. Closing the page folds it back onto the shelf.

BWhy it is his

His work is supply-chain systems: things that are packed, labelled, routed and checked. Each project is a delivered unit with one honest number on the label.

CMain risk

Charm over clarity. Six cartons read as a toy if the labels are not crisp, and the unfold must stay under a second or it delays the reading.
