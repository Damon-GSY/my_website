What the visitor does

Scrolls and watches a light swing around a knot of wire. Noise, noise, then one exact angle where the shadow snaps into his name, then into his work.

Why it is his

His job is finding the angle where a mess becomes legible: a trace, a benchmark, a failure mode. The hero acts it out instead of saying it.

Main risk

The wire must be solved per wordmark and per diagram, and it only works near the locked angle. Mobile and reduced motion need the flat wordmark first.
